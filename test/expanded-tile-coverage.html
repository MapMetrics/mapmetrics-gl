About Expanded Tile Coverage

This feature loads additional tiles around the current viewport to provide smoother panning and zooming experience. The buffer size determines how many tile rows/columns are loaded beyond the visible area.

  • Buffer 0: Only visible tiles (default behavior)
  • Buffer 1: One tile row/column around the perimeter
  • Buffer 2: Two tile rows/columns around the perimeter

Try panning and zooming to see the difference in loading behavior!
