Direct Zoom vs Animated Zoom:

  • Direct Zoom (setZoom): Instantly jumps to the target zoom level without animation
  • Animated Zoom (easeTo): Smoothly animates to the target zoom level over time

In the scroll zoom handler, we now use setZoom() for immediate zoom changes instead of easeTo().
